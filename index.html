How to Play Sky Hopper

🕹️ Controls & Sky Wrapping

Your Hopper automatically bounces upward every time you land on an island. Your goal is to climb as high as possible without falling below the clouds!

  • Desktop: Hold ← / → or A / D to steer left and right in mid-air.
  • Mobile / Touch: Use the hold the left or right half of the screen (or use the faint arrow hints).
  • Screen Wrap: Flying off the far-left edge wraps you smoothly around to the far-right edge (and vice versa)—use this to reach distant islands!

❤️ Hearts & Instant Revive

Keep an eye out for glowing 3D Hearts floating above islands:

  • Double Reward: Every Heart you collect grants +1 ❤️ revive. Hearts do not add score, and you can hold up to 3.
  • Automatic Second Chance: If you miss a jump and fall while holding at least 1 Heart, you revive immediately! One Heart is spent to summon a rescue boost that launches you right back into the action.

✨ Golden Score Orbs

Gold orbs add a randomly rolled 20–1000 points to your score. Rare 500 and 1000 point rolls shine brighter!

🏝️ Sky Islands & Boosters

🟢 Emerald Turf Islands

Solid, reliable floating islands. Safe to bounce on as many times as you need.

🔵 Glider Platforms

Motorized sky pads that patrol horizontally across the clouds. Time your landing!

🟠 Cracked Sandstone

Appears at higher altitudes! Gives you one full bounce before shattering into dust.

🚀 Gold Boost Springs

Launches your Hopper into a high-speed somersault past multiple islands at once.
